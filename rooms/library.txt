THE LIBRARY

Floor to ceiling shelves line every wall. Most of the books have
rotted beyond recognition, their pages turned to dust.

One shelf catches your eye. A single book sits upright, untouched by
decay. Its spine reads: "The Whitmore Family History, 1850-1923."

You flip through the pages. Names, dates, births, deaths. The final
chapter is titled "The Disappearance" but every page after the title
has been torn out.

A cold draft blows through the room. The book slams shut on its own.

Exits: FOYER (west), BALLROOM (north)
