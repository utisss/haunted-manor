THE HIDDEN CHAMBER

Behind the false wall in the ballroom, a narrow passage leads down
a spiral staircase into darkness.

At the bottom, you find a small stone chamber lit by an unknown source.
In the center sits a pedestal with a single piece of parchment.

The writing is still legible:

    "To whoever finds this, the Whitmore secret dies with me.
     But if you have made it this far, you deserve the truth.

     utflag{d34d_c0mm1ts_t3ll_n0_t4l3s}

     Guard it well."

                                        - E. Whitmore, 1923
