THE BALLROOM

A vast room with vaulted ceilings and tall windows, all boarded shut.

A grand piano sits in the corner. As you approach, a single key
presses down on its own, releasing a mournful note that echoes
through the empty hall.

The dance floor is covered in a thin layer of dust, except for a
circle in the center where the dust has been disturbed. As if
someone, or something, has been dancing here recently.

You notice scratch marks on the floor near the north wall. They
seem to point toward a section of the wallpaper that is slightly
discolored. Behind it, you find nothing but cold stone.

Exits: FOYER (south), LIBRARY (south)
