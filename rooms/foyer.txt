THE FOYER

You push open the heavy oak doors. The hinges scream in protest.

The grand foyer stretches before you, marble floors cracked and stained.
A chandelier hangs overhead, swaying gently despite the still air.
Cobwebs drape every surface like funeral veils.

A guest book sits on a pedestal near the door. The last entry is dated
October 31st, 1923. The ink has faded but you can still make out the words:
"We should never have come here."

Exits: LIBRARY (east), BALLROOM (north)
